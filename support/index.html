aili@dynamistripe.com

Report a problem

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the problem. Screenshots can help, but remove private information first. Never send your password or payment details.

Manage a subscription

On your device, open Settings → your Apple account → Subscriptions. To restore an existing purchase, use Restore Purchases on the app's plans screen. Request refunds through Apple's Report a Problem service.

Remove your data

For on-device use, reset the study record in Profile. For account mode, choose Account → Delete account. This removes the account's study data and uploaded files. Deleting data or uninstalling the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Understand readiness

Initial topic estimates come from the experience level selected during setup. Recorded answers then update those estimates. Readiness is a study-planning signal and never a guarantee of an exam result.

Personalization

Personalization adapts the presentation of explanations. It does not change grading, correctness, or mastery calculations. You can disable it and remove personalization data in the app.

Account access

If your version includes accounts, the sign-in screen offers password reset by email. If the recovery link does not complete successfully, contact support rather than sharing your password.
