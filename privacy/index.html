FINANCIER

Privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026

This policy describes data handled by Financier, the finance exam-preparation app operated by Dynamistripe LLC ("we", "us"). Available features depend on the version and configuration of the app you use.

Using Financier on your device

Without an account, your first name, exam selection, study schedule, practice answers, confidence, timing, study estimates, and tutor conversation are stored on your device. Optional personalization facts are also stored locally. The on-device tutor uses fixed Financier reference lessons; these messages are not sent to a remote AI service.

You can clear the local study record using the reset control in Profile. Device backups are controlled by your Apple settings.

Account and study data

When account features are available and you create an account, we use your email address, first name, account identifier, enrollment details, and recorded study activity to sign you in, save your progress, provide feedback, and calculate mastery. Account services use Supabase for authentication, database storage, and file storage, and Resend to deliver account emails such as sign-up confirmation and password reset.

Practice answers, confidence choices, and response times help determine what you should study next. Readiness and mastery figures are study estimates, not exam-result predictions.

Optional personalization

Personalization is optional. You can provide study-relevant background information and, when account document processing is enabled, upload a document or paste text for extraction. You review extracted facts before activating them. Personalization changes how explanations are presented; it does not change correct answers or your scores.

A LinkedIn URL may be stored as a reference. The app does not fetch or scrape that page. Account document extraction uses OpenAI through a server request when enabled. Before the first document is sent, the app tells you that its text goes to OpenAI and asks for your permission; you can decline and add facts yourself, and you can withdraw permission on the Personalization screen. The text is sent as written, so remove anything you would rather not share. Your account email and account identifier are not added to that request. Original uploads are removed after processing unless you select retention; confirmed facts and source metadata remain until you delete them. Your documents are not included in the on-device tutor's replies.

You can turn personalization off and remove individual sources or all personalization data in the app. If remote tutor capabilities are introduced, the disclosure and consent presented with those features will identify the provider and information shared before use.

Subscriptions and reminders

Apple processes in-app purchases and subscriptions. Financier reads subscription entitlement status; it does not receive your full card or bank-payment details. Study reminders are local notifications and are enabled only with your permission.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell your personal data.

Retention and deletion

Local study information stays on your device until you reset it or remove its app data. In account mode, you can choose Account → Delete account to remove the active account, its associated study and personalization records, and uploaded files. Deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage that separately through Apple.

Service-provider backup retention is subject to the applicable provider's policies. We do not promise immediate removal from every backup. You may contact us about access or deletion requests.

Where your information is processed

Financier is operated from the United States. Account information is stored and processed in the United States by the service providers named below. If you use Financier from another country, your information is transferred to the United States, where privacy law may differ from the law where you live.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information tied to your account, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Account deletion is also available in the app at any time. Email the address under "Contact" from the address on your account; we answer within 30 days and never charge for a request or treat you differently for making one.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, such as objecting to or restricting certain processing, or complaining to a data protection authority. We process account information to provide the service you asked for, personalization information with your consent, and limited technical information to keep the service secure.

Security and service providers

Account requests use encrypted transport. Database access controls limit access to learner-owned records; account practice grading is performed by the server. No method of transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

Supabase, Resend, Apple, and OpenAI process information for the features described above under their applicable service and privacy terms. Only information needed for the feature is provided. Do not include passwords, bank account numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information in uploads or tutor messages.

Children

Financier is intended for professional exam preparation and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information and we will delete it.

Policy changes

We will update this page and its effective date when this policy changes. We will provide additional notice or seek consent where required.
